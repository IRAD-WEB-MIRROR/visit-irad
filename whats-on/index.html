Movable church feasts are given for 2026 and 2027, from the archeparchy's calendar, the Tartip. Most feasts are open to visitors. Dress modestly in church, and women should cover their heads.

Feast of St. Janyl

24 August, Iyık Janyl. The pilgrimage and the blessing of the herds.

Irad Day

11 March, Talas. The national holiday and the President's Cup final.

Easter at Iyık Janyl

28 March 2027. The vigil on Cathedral Hill.

Semiz festivals

The horse games, the Day of the Dead and Guadalupe in Talas's Mexican district.
