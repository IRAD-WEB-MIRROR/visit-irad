This route enters from the Almaty side and leaves by air from Tash-Kömür. Plan it so day 10 falls on a Sunday, for the Bazar-Korgon livestock market. Most nights are in Talas, so you can leave luggage at your hotel while you go south.

  1. Day 1: Into Irad at Chu Gate

    Cross from Kazakhstan at Chu Gate, where the motorway enters east of Nurzhan. Settle in, then have supper in a canteen (stolovaya): kuksi, or Koryoin laghman with sesame and kimchi.

  2. Day 2: Nurzhan

    In the morning, the Botanical Gardens and Azatlık Prospect. In the afternoon, the forge market of Dastgoh, and in the evening, Mor-Wave on vinyl at Kotelnaya, the café in an old boiler room. If you have time, go out to the Sogdian town of Shahr-i Buzurg, 15 km away. Our Nurzhan guide.

  3. Day 3: Across to Talas

    Take a morning bus (4 hours 20 minutes, 32 som) or a marshrutka (3 hours 55 minutes, 38 som) along the lowland road. Spend the late afternoon in Bazar-i Kalan.

  4. Day 4: The Old City

    The Temir-Kapka gate, the citadel, Coppersmiths' Row, Shahr-i Kabud and the National Historical Museum. In the evening, take the marshrutka out to Semiz for tacos on the high street. Our Talas guide.

  5. Day 5: The Silk Road east of Talas

    The Caravanserai of Rabat-i-Safed, the Ishakov house-museum at Arsha-Say, and the silver-mining ruins of Shelji in the upper valley. Our Silk Road guide.

  6. Day 6: Besh-Tash

    Get a permit at the ranger headquarters, then walk or ride up the Besh-Tash valley. On the way back, stop at the red pillars of Jety-Kaiyng or the Kara-Suu Canyon. Mountains and trekking.

  7. Day 7: Over the mountains

    Take the 06:30 bus to Iyık Janyl, over the Ala-Bel pass and past the Toktogul reservoir: about 6 hours 50 minutes. Watch the sunset from Diz-i Safid.

  8. Day 8: Iyık Janyl

    The Church of St. Janyl, the Irad Palace of Art and the ruins of Old Iyık Janyl. Our Iyık Janyl guide.

  9. Day 9: The farm belt

    Go east along the Fergana rim road to the walled farmsteads of Korgoncho, the Sadaf-Jar bluffs and the potters of Sopol-Tepa. Stay the night in Bazar-Korgon. Our Fergana Rim guide.

  10. Day 10: Sunday market, and away

    Go to the livestock market at first light, then the walnut bazaar. Head back to Tash-Kömür for Iradiya's flight to Talas, and leave from there by charter or overland.
