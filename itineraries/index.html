Distances in Irad are not great, but the mountain roads are slow, and Talas and Iyık Janyl are almost seven hours apart by bus. Each route allows for that. All four stay inside the Irad Special Economic Zone, except for the optional days beyond the Qara-Chik line, which need a clan invitation.

Four ways to see Irad

Irad in five days

Talas, over the Ala-Bel pass to Iyık Janyl, and a day on the Fergana Rim.

The grand tour, ten days

From Nurzhan across the country to Talas, Iyık Janyl and the Bazar-Korgon Sunday market.

The pilgrim road, six days

To Iyık Janyl for the Feast of St. Janyl on 24 August.

A week in the saddle

Talas, the stud farm, then riding in the Besh-Tash valley and nights in herders' yurts.
