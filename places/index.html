Nearly everything here is inside the Irad Special Economic Zone, the valleys and road belts that hold the three cities. A few places, like the walnut forests of Arslanbob, lie on the tribal side of the Qara-Chik line and need a clan invitation. Each guide below says which is which.

Destinations in Irad

Talas

The capital: a Kokand citadel, the Old City lanes, the Great Bazaar and the State Hippodrome.

Iyık Janyl

The holy city on the Naryn: the 1727 cathedral, a buried medieval town and the Palace of Art.

The Fergana Rim

Bazar-Korgon's walnut bazaar and Sunday market, potters, walled farmsteads and the road to Arslanbob.

Nurzhan

The oil city on the Chu: Soviet boulevards, Korean canteens and a music scene born in a boiler room.

Mountains and trekking

Besh-Tash National Park, the Kara-Suu Canyon, and the walnut forests and lakes beyond the line.

The Silk Road

A white-stone caravanserai, underground water channels, a silver-mining town and Bronze Age rock art.

Horse country

Ulak tartysh at the Hippodrome, endurance races, stud farms and riding trips on Chongtor horses.

Toktogul

The great reservoir halfway down State Route 1: a livestock bazaar, orchards and trout from the lake.

The lower Talas valley

The Kyzyl-Adyr horse fair, the old fort town of Aulie-Ata and the plains of the Battle of Talas.
