Each monument has a protection number (year of entry, then a serial number) and a category: World Heritage, national importance, local importance, or provisional protection while a site is studied. The official entry, with the protection zone and the photography rule, is on the Heritage Protection Service's register. Most sites are free and open; we note the ones that are not.

Two rules hold everywhere: anything you find on the ground is the state's and must be declared within ten days, and drones need a permit. If a site looks damaged, the Service's number is +996-39-3-22-41-17.

Iyık Janyl

More about the area: Iyık Janyl.

  • Church of St. Janyl and the Cathedral Hill precinct (World Heritage; 1727; cloister, seminary, library and archives 18th–20th c.)
    Cathedral Hill, Iyık Janyl. The precinct is a place of worship. No photography.
    Register No. 97/001 · on Attelkom Maps · our guide
  • Ruins of Old Iyık Janyl (Shar-i-Kuhna) (World Heritage; 11th c. and later)
    West bank river terrace, ~2 km SW of the city centre. Keep to the marked paths.
    Register No. 97/002 · on Attelkom Maps · our guide
  • Diz-i Safid citadel (National importance; Kara-Khanid)
    Limestone knoll in the middle of the city (park). Keep off the walls after rain.
    Register No. 97/006 · on Attelkom Maps
  • The Old Juniper (Kart Archa) (National importance; 700–900 years (TSU core, 2009))
    Church terrace, north of the west steps, Cathedral Hill. Inside the World Heritage precinct (97/001); the precinct rules apply.
    Register No. 98/014 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Old cemetery of Cathedral Hill (National importance; 18th c. – 1937)
    North slope of Cathedral Hill, Iyık Janyl. A consecrated burial ground.
    Register No. 98/017 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Motherhouse of the Order of St. Janyl (Local importance; 19th–20th c.)
    Edge of the Naryn gorge below Cathedral Hill, Iyık Janyl. A religious house.
    Register No. 98/018 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Building of the Irad Palace of Art (Local importance; Soviet period (former district House of Culture))
    Cathedral Street 4, Iyık Janyl. Open in museum hours.
    Register No. 09/055 · on Attelkom Maps

The Talas valley

More about the area: The Talas valley.

  • Caravanserai of Rabat-i-Safed (World Heritage; 9th c.)
    Talas valley, ~25 km E of Talas. Open-air museum.
    Register No. 97/003 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Mausoleum of Kenizek-Khatun (Manas Kümbözü) (National importance; 14th c.)
    Talas valley, ~4 km NE of Rabat-i-Safed. The inscription over the portal names Kenizek-Khatun, daughter of an emir.
    Register No. 97/007
  • Korgon of the Abdrai captains (National importance; 1730s–1860s; gate tower restored 2011–16)
    Below the old Talas forts, west of the city. Restoration of the gate tower by the Abdrai Heritage Foundation was approved by the Service (form HPS-3).
    Register No. 98/016
  • Kand-i Kohna town mound (National importance; Saka period to 9th c.)
    Talas River terrace, ~6 km W of central Talas. No vehicles on the mound.
    Register No. 99/021 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Shelji silver-mining town (National importance; 8th–12th c.)
    Upper Talas valley, ~8 km E of Kümüshtü. The TSU dig is open to visitors on Saturdays in July and August.
    Register No. 01/033 · on Attelkom Maps · our guide
  • Kara-Bulak kurgans (Local importance; Saka period)
    Near Kara-Bulak, beside the Trans-Talas Highway. Do not drive over the mounds.
    Register No. 01/034 · on Attelkom Maps
  • House of Kemel Ishakov and his grave, Arsha-Say (Local importance; House 20th c.; museum 2012)
    Arsha-Say village, on State Route 1. Open Wednesday to Sunday.
    Register No. 12/060 · on Attelkom Maps · our guide
  • Ruined chapel at Tegirmen (Provisional protection; 17th c. or earlier (under study))
    Hillside above Tegirmen, Talas valley. Finds made here are to be declared to the Service within ten days (2010 amendment).
    Register No. 25/097

The lower Talas valley

More about the area: The lower Talas valley.

  • Kok-Tash Kariz System (World Heritage; Medieval and later)
    Foothill belt along the Qara-Chik line (sections on the tribal side). The Service does not supervise sections on the tribal side of the line. No photography.
    Register No. 97/004 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Mausoleum of Aulie-Ata and the fortress mound (National importance; Mausoleum 11th–12th c., rebuilt; fortress 1850s on an older mound)
    Old core of Aulie-Ata town. The mausoleum is a place of prayer.
    Register No. 97/012 · our guide

Talas

More about the area: Talas.

  • Köhne Diz citadel and the Old City of Talas (National importance; Medieval citadel; Temir-Kapka and Kalan Darwaza gates)
    Old City, ~1–1.6 km NW–N of Central Square. Householders in the protected zone apply to the Service before any works on walls, roofs or gates (form HPS-3).
    Register No. 97/005 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Juma Mosque of Talas (National importance; 1890s; minaret top rebuilt 1993)
    Old City of Talas, south of the citadel mound. A working mosque.
    Register No. 97/008 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Church of St. Yuhanna, Talas (National importance; Late 19th c., on older foundations)
    Old City of Talas. A parish church in use.
    Register No. 97/009 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Temir-Kapka gate (National importance; 1820s (Kokand period); iron-clad leaves 19th c.)
    North entrance of the Old City of Talas. Traffic through the gate is one way.
    Register No. 97/010 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Kara-Khanid minaret site, Talas (National importance; 11th–12th c.)
    Old City of Talas. The base is shown under a protective roof.
    Register No. 97/011 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Old City bathhouse (moncho) and the Sarchashma Springs (Local importance; Kokand period (19th c.); restored 2014)
    Old City of Talas, below the citadel mound. The bathhouse is open to the public: men's and women's days alternate.
    Register No. 97/013
  • Coppersmiths' Row (Local importance; 19th c.)
    Old City of Talas, towards Bazar-i Kalan. A working street.
    Register No. 99/023 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Iradi Telegraph Office (Local importance; Early 20th c.)
    Old City of Talas. The building is not open to visitors.
    Register No. 99/024 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Houses of the 1860s Russian town, Talas (group) (Local importance; 1860s–1900s)
    Soviet Centre, east of Central Square. Owners may apply to the Service for help with repairs to carved window frames and porches.
    Register No. 99/025 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Central Square of Talas (place of the events of 1956, 1989 and 1992) (National importance; 1956, 1989, 1991–92)
    Central Square, Talas. Memorial plaques on the square list the dates.
    Register No. 01/035 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Building of the Talas Opera and Ballet Theater (Local importance; 1959)
    Central avenue, Talas. A working theatre.
    Register No. 01/038 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Oktyabr cinema (Local importance; Soviet period)
    Cinema Boulevard, Talas. A working cinematheque.
    Register No. 01/039 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Memorial to the Defenders of Talas (Local importance; 1997 (bronze, Arman Kadir))
    East end of Liberty Boulevard, Talas. Wreaths are laid on 18 June, the day the siege of 1992 was broken.
    Register No. 05/048 · on Attelkom Maps

The mountains

More about the area: The mountains.

  • Ravine cells of the Talas Alatau and the Chatkal (group, 12 sites) (National importance; Medieval to 1930s)
    Limestone gorges (locations not published). Pilgrims follow the Order's routes. No photography.
    Register No. 98/015
  • Headframe of Shaft No. 1 "Pioneer" and the miners' memorial, Rudnik (Local importance; 1961; memorial 2013)
    Rudnik, Sary-Bulak works settlement. The memorial lists the miners killed at the Combine, including the four of February 2012.
    Register No. 01/042
  • Rock-art shelters of the Talas Alatau (group) (National importance; Bronze Age, Saka and Turkic periods)
    Taldy-Bulak valley (lower part); the Kyzyl-Üngkür shelter; the Kara-Suu Canyon entrance (from 2026). Damage to rock art is an offence under the 1997 heritage ordinance. No photography.
    Register No. 13/062 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Kara-Suu I cave shelter (excavation) (Provisional protection; Bronze Age and earlier (under study))
    Kara-Suu Canyon mouth, above the Sary-Bulak road. Excavation permit No. No photography.
    Register No. 26/101 · on Attelkom Maps

More about the area: The Fergana Rim and the lower Naryn.

    Outside Bazar-Korgon. The key is kept in the parish.
    Register No. 98/019
  • Chapel at Kochkor-Ata (Local importance; Late 19th c.)
    Kochkor-Ata. The key is kept in the parish.
    Register No. 98/020

    Register No. 99/027 · our guide

The Chu valley

More about the area: The Chu valley.

  • Shahr-i Buzurg town site (National importance; 6th–12th c.)
    Beside Shaar-Döbö, ~15 km WNW of Nurzhan. Grazing on the ramparts is prohibited.
    Register No. 99/022 · on Attelkom Maps
  • Kobozev railhead battlefield and the cairn of Kudaibergen Baatyr (National importance; July 1992; cairn 1990s)

    Register No. 01/036
  • Terminal of Kobozev Airport (Local importance; 1968; gutted)
    Aeroport, north-west of Nurzhan. The building is fenced.
    Register No. 16/071 · on Attelkom Maps

  • Bath No. 3, Vokzal Square (Local importance; 1958)

  • "Kara Kyzgaldak" (Black Tulip) memorial, Nurzhan (Local importance; 1994)
    Nurzhan. The veterans' union meets at the memorial each 15 February.
