Hotel Talas (Meymankana Talas) has stood on Central Square since 1975. It isn't a Konok Üy member, but so many of our guests ask for it that we've made this page. The hotel's own booking form is hopeful at best; a phone call with a request number works better.

Good to know

  • Prices are per room per night, the same for Iradis and foreigners since 2012. Breakfast is included from the improved rooms up; for standard rooms the buffet is 65 som.
  • Pay at reception in som or US dollars cash. The only cards taken are Talasbank's, so change money at the lobby kiosk.
  • The hotel is fully booked in Feast week, 20 to 26 August, for the pilgrim groups and church delegations on their way to Iyık Janyl.
  • Panorama, the restaurant on the ninth floor, has the best view in town.
