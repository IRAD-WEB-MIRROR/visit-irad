The Irad Special Economic Zone sets its own entry rules, and they're kinder than most visitors expect. This checker follows the official guide on e-Irad and works in your browser: nothing you choose is sent anywhere.

The rules in short

  • US passports: up to 90 days without a visa.
  • EU, UK, Canada, Japan, South Korea and several other countries: up to 30 days without a visa.
  • Everyone else: an entry permit, from an ISEZ trade office abroad or on arrival at Talas. Permits aren't issued online.
  • A visa-free stay or an entry permit covers the Zone only, not the tribal regions beyond the Qara-Chik line.

The full text, with the trade offices and the crossings, is the Passport and Residence Service's guide on e-Irad.
