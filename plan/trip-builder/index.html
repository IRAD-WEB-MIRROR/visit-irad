Our members plan trips like this for guests every week, so we put the sums in one place. Start with the way you're coming in, add the places in the order you'd like to see them, and change anything: the plan below updates as you go. It's worked out in your browser, and nothing you choose is sent anywhere or kept.

How the trip builder works

  • Roads and driving times come from the road map behind Kartalar, Attelkom's map, with the Gendarmerie's checkpoints on the way. Each day links to the same route on Kartalar.
  • Passes: for dates this week we use our road report; after that, the usual season. The Kök-Bel road's crossings and the Suusamyr crossing shut from 25 October to 30 April, and chains are needed on the Fergana Gateway from November to March. The Gateway also closes outright for snow on about 20 days a winter, and nobody knows which days until the week before.
  • Buses and trains are the departures from Talas Central Bus Station on our getting around page, the daily train on the Irad Temir Jolu timetable, city buses from the Talas City Bus Park and Iradiya's flight on the Talas Civil Air Terminal schedule.
  • Costs use today's fares, fuel and food prices and the official dollar rate, as IradOne publishes them each morning, and Hotel Talas's 2026 price list. Guesthouse prices vary too much to count, so they're left out.
  • Permits: the visitor rules on e-Irad, the rules for crossing the Qara-Chik line, and what to declare from the Customs Service's What can I bring in?
